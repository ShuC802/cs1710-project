Definitions, data coverage and caveats
  • Data: LLM Price-Performance Tracker (Kaggle), scrape of 2026-03-31, 453 model endpoints. Nothing is imputed; every chart says how many models it had to leave out.
  • Good enough: score ≥ X% of the best score any model has in that task (best: general 57.2, coding 57.3, math 99.0). The three indexes use different scales, so a percentage gives one shared definition.
  • Monthly bill: usage tokens × the model's input and output list prices at a 3:1 input:output mix (the same mix the dataset's blended price uses).
  • Release year only: the dataset has no release dates and 161 models have no year, so time views are year cohorts and skip those models. The 138 models without a price (mostly open-weight, lower scoring) cannot appear in any price view.
  • Free models are open-weight models with a $0 list price. They are shown in a separate lane and never used for "× cheaper".
