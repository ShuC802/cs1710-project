One line per release year. For any score on the x-axis, the line shows the price of the cheapest paid model from that year that scores at least that much. Data. LLM Price-Performance Tracker (Kaggle), 453 model endpoints, scrape of 2026-03-31. The tracker reports only a release year, so time views group models by release year. 138 models have no price and 161 have no year; each view says what it leaves out. Nothing is imputed. Cleaning steps: dashboards/data/cleaning_log.txt.

Prices are list prices from the 2026-03-31 snapshot and may have changed since. Reasoning models often emit more tokens, so real bills can run higher.
